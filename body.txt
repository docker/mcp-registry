Adds VibeFix as a remote server (streamable-http, `https://vibe-fixer.com/mcp`).

VibeFix triages broken web apps built with AI app builders (Lovable, Bolt, v0, Base44, Replit): the most likely cause of a symptom, the evidence that would confirm it, and what a fix involves. The public tool `assess_broken_app` is read-only and needs no authentication.

- Docs / repo: https://github.com/yos88da/vibefix-mcp
- Official MCP Registry: `com.vibe-fixer/vibefix`
- Files: `servers/vibefix/server.yaml`, `readme.md`, `tools.json` (`[]`, dynamic discovery), following the remote-server section of CONTRIBUTING.
