Spottable searches Facebook Marketplace, opens listing details and photos, and analyzes potential deals through a hosted, read-only MCP server.

Connect using Streamable HTTP at https://api.spottableapp.com/v1/mcp and sign in to Spottable through OAuth. A paid Spottable Pro or Max plan is required. Marketplace searches, listing lookups, and fresh analyses use the account's shared Spottable credits.

Spottable cannot contact sellers or purchase items. It does not require a Facebook login and is not affiliated with Facebook or Meta.
