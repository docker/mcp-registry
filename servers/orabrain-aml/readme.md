# Prax Data Services — Orabrain AML

Screen banks, wires, sanctions, wallets and transfers before an agent moves money. Free tier.

Prax Data Services provides AML, sanctions screening, counterparty risk, and threat intelligence through MCP, enabling AI agents and applications to check risk before taking financial actions.

## Capabilities

- Bank and counterparty screening
- Wire and transfer screening
- Sanctions and watchlist screening
- Crypto wallet risk screening
- Blockchain and illicit-fund exposure analysis
- Company and entity screening
- Adverse media and enforcement intelligence
- Country and jurisdiction risk
- Domain, IP and threat intelligence
- Vulnerability and software supply-chain checks
