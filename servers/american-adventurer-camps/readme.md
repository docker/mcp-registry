# American Adventurer Camps: National Forest & BLM Camping

National forest and BLM camping records for AI agents. Find free and dispersed camps on US public land, read one camp's full record, plan a four-night trip, check gas and water, get the National Weather Service forecast, and ask whose land a point is (national forests). Every answer gives the record's link, its source and the date the data was built. Read-only, no sign-in, no key.
