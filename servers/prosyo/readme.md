[Prosyo](https://prosyo.com) is AI LinkedIn and email outreach. Connect it to any MCP client to work with your real Prosyo prospects, campaigns and replies:

- Find, research and enrich prospects, and see who is showing buying intent
- Draft LinkedIn and email campaigns from your ideal customer profile, then preview, launch, pause or resume them
- Read your inbox, draft replies and answer prospects
- Check how each campaign is performing

Sign in with your Prosyo account and choose the workspace to connect. New workspaces start with a 7-day free trial.
