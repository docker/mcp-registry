# Product Metrics

Product Metrics is a profit optimisation platform for ecommerce advertising: one app for server-side tracking, profit bidding, product segmentation and campaign planning across Google Ads, Meta and ChatGPT Ads.

Connect the hosted MCP server at https://www.productmetrics.io/mcp and sign in through OAuth. Connect your Product Metrics account, or create one when you sign in. Connecting data is free; paid product features use a subscription.
