# Hail

Give your AI assistant a phone number and an email inbox.

Let your assistant handle calls, texts, and email. Building an app? Add AI
calls, email and SMS with one API.

Connect Docker MCP Toolkit to Hail to make and take calls, send and receive
messages, and check conversation history. Get phone numbers in 120+
countries, with help for local rules and required documents, including US
A2P 10DLC registration. Choose a built-in voice or bring your own.

Use the hosted service with OAuth. Build with the API, CLI, or Python SDK.
Open-source ([AGPL-3.0-or-later](https://github.com/hail-hq/hail)) or cloud
priced on usage. Refer to the [MCP setup guide](https://hail.so/mcp) to connect.

Start with a read-only request:

```text
Find call-capable Hail numbers in the UK, France, and Canada. Compare
monthly fees, setup fees, and registration requirements.
```
