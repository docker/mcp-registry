ForthClear is a B2B marketplace for surplus, overstock and returned inventory. The catalogue tools work without an account: search live lots by keyword or by meaning and open full product details. After an OAuth sign-in, buyers can request and track bulk quotes, check out eligible listings and follow their orders, and sellers can manage listings, answer quote requests and mark orders shipped. Stripe processes payments and the seller is the seller of record.

Endpoint: https://app.forthclear.io/mcp (Streamable HTTP)
Auth: none for catalogue tools; OAuth (buyer and seller scopes) for account actions
