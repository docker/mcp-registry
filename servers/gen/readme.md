# GEN

GEN is the creative AI video engineer for ads, cartoons, AI UGC and microdramas. Research what works, create and edit video, and publish through the hosted MCP.

Use a GEN personal access token (PAT) for this Docker connection. Create a free account and mint a token at https://gen.pro/api, or follow the autonomous agent registration instructions at https://gen.pro/skill.md. Configure it as the GEN_PAT secret; do not place it in the endpoint URL.

The hosted MCP also supports OAuth in compatible clients. Anonymous initialization and discovery do not grant access to private account data or paid operations.

Video editing and social publishing are free. AI generation requires credits, funded by credit card or x402.
