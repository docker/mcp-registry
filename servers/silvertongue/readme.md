Silvertongue's hosted MCP gives agents famous writing voices to write with. Each voice is a researched study of how someone writes, drawn from what they published, with every source linked. An agent searches voices by name, lane or what the writing should do, reads a voice's profile, sources and limits, and takes its instructions for a post, reply, explanation or thread at a chosen length, platform and edge. Every VOICE.md is a resource, and the write_as prompt switches voice and drafts in one command.

Connect to https://mcp.silvertongue.dev/mcp using streamable HTTP. It is free and read-only and needs no authentication; the agent's own model does the writing.

Voices are unofficial studies of public writing technique. Nobody named wrote or endorsed them, and drafts are never presented as their words.

Source repository: https://github.com/auscaster/silvertongue
