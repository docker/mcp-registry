# TreppenliftVergleich

Answers questions about stairlifts and home lifts in Germany with the same calculators and fact register as treppenliftvergleichsportal.de: the price range including installation by lift type, stair shape and floors, minus the long-term care insurance subsidy (§ 40 (4) SGB XI); federal, state and city grants with their current status; whether a lift fits a staircase of a given width (DIN 18065, MVV TB); what to check when a stairlift beeps or stops, with brand notes and documented error codes; and whether a repair is still worth it. Every figure comes with its source and check date. Answers are in German. It never asks for names, addresses or health data and stores nothing.

## Tools

- `treppenlift_kosten_schaetzen`: price range and own share after the subsidy
- `foerderung_finden`: grants and loans by federal state and city
- `treppe_pruefen`: does a lift fit this stair width
- `treppenlift_stoerung`: checks for beeping, stopping and error codes
- `reparieren_oder_ersetzen`: repair quote versus new price
- `treppenlift_fakten`: sourced facts by topic

All tools are read-only. The server is public: no account, no login and no API key.
