Forthcast gives an agent inventory planning tools. Four calculators work with no account: reorder point, order quantity, a demand forecast from a sales series, and an inventory health score, plus plain explanations of each metric. A Shopify merchant who signs in with OAuth (read-only scope) also lets the agent read their live inventory overview, reorder suggestions, items, purchase orders and supplier delivery performance. The connector cannot change stock, settings or purchase orders.

Endpoint: https://app.forthcast.io/mcp (Streamable HTTP)
Auth: none for calculators; OAuth (inventory:read) for store data
Publisher: Forthsuite (Hylke Reitsma), info@forthcast.io
Registry name: io.forthcast/inventory
