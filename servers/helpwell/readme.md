# HelpWell

[HelpWell](https://helpwell.ai), by Workado, LLC, connects AI assistants to your help desk, support ticketing software, knowledge base, help docs, live chat support, contacts, email marketing, and marketing automation for SaaS.

## Connect

- Endpoint: `https://app.helpwell.ai/mcp`
- Transport: Streamable HTTP
- Authentication: OAuth 2.1 authorization code with PKCE. Sign in to HelpWell, choose a workspace, and approve the access level and tool areas you want to enable.
- [Connection and setup documentation](https://docs.helpwell.ai/getting-started/ai-assistants)

Tools are discovered dynamically and depend on your approved workspace, access level, tool areas, role, and plan. A HelpWell account is required. Email delivery uses your configured Resend integration; Stripe workflows depend on your configured integration. You can revoke an assistant's access in HelpWell settings.

## Example tasks

- Summarize my help desk inbox and draft replies for the oldest support tickets without sending them.
- Find unanswered live chat questions and draft knowledge base articles to address the gaps.
- Review support desk response times and unresolved conversations over the last 30 days.
- Draft an email marketing newsletter from release notes and preview its eligible audience.
- Design a SaaS onboarding journey and test the email marketing automation before publishing.
- Check our Resend email setup and find verified help docs for Stripe billing questions.
