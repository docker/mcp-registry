# CoworkingView

A hosted, read-only MCP server for searching real coworking spaces, private offices, desks, meeting rooms and day passes, with the prices operators publish, in Berlin, Madrid, Barcelona, London, Munich, Dubai and 70+ other cities.

- No API key, no sign-up: Streamable HTTP endpoint `https://mcp.coworkingview.com/mcp`.
- Read-only: nothing books, pays or contacts anyone.
- Market rates (median, quartiles, sample size) per workspace type for a city or country, licensed CC BY 4.0.
- Public holidays and bridge days for Germany, Spain and the UK.

## Try asking

- "Find a private office for 6 people in Berlin Mitte under €3,000 a month."
- "What does a flex desk cost in Madrid compared with Barcelona?"
- "Which WeWork locations are there in London, and what do they charge for a dedicated desk?"
