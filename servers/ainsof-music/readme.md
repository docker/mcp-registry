# AINSOF

Original, human-made production music inside your AI assistant. Search the AINSOF
catalog by written brief or by reference link (YouTube, Spotify, Apple Music or Deezer;
for SoundCloud and TikTok give the artist and title), hear complete clean previews,
cut a cue to any length, fetch versions, stems and cue sheets, and score video
straight to picture. 16 tools.

- Documentation: https://github.com/AINSOF-Tech/mcp
- Website: https://ainsof.io
- Endpoint: https://mcp.ainsof.io (no key required to start; video tools ask for a one-time sign-in on ainsof.io)
