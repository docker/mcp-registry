# Intertwine House Plans Plan Finder

A read-only remote MCP server for the coastal and Southern house plans residential designer Angela Kamer sells direct at [intertwinehouseplans.com](https://intertwinehouseplans.com): 68 plan sets, 57 homes and 11 garage apartment and ADU plans, most of them built for coastal flood and hurricane zones.

## Tools

- `search_plans`: search by bedrooms, baths, heated square feet, stories, width and depth, elevated or pilings foundation, elevator, drive-under garage, in-law suite, garage ADU, style and price
- `get_plan`: one plan in full, by name or its other published names, with photos, every package and its current price, the study set and the design write-up
- `plans_for_lot`: plans whose footprint fits a lot after setbacks
- `list_features`: styles and features with plan counts
- `about_designer`: who draws the plans and what a plan set comes as
- `buying_help`: what a plan set includes, study sets, changes, cost to build, built homes and the FAQ

No account or key is needed. Prices and photos are read live from the store, and every result links to the plan page.
