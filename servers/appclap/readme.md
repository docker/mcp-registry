# AppClap MCP Server

AppClap is an app store for web apps that your AI assistant can search. Describe what you need in plain language and get apps ranked purely by how well they fit, including small and often cheaper ones, with prices, free plans and features read from each vendor's own website and the date they were checked. No key or sign-in is needed.

## Tools

- **search_apps** – Ranked web apps for a plain-language need ("a free form builder", "an open-source alternative to Notion", "a CRM under $20/month").
- **get_app** – The full record for one app: pricing in the vendor's own currency and plans, features, platforms, open-source and self-hosting status.
- **compare_apps** – Two to five apps side by side: pricing, free plan, trial, open source, shared and distinct features.
- **submit_app** – Add a web app to the catalog; it is verified against the app's own site before it appears.
- **report_app_issue** – Flag a wrong or outdated detail on a listed app; it triggers a re-check.

## Example Use Cases

- Find a free, open-source alternative to Notion that can be self-hosted.
- Pick a form builder under $10 a month with a free plan.
- Compare Umami and Plausible on pricing and self-hosting.
