# Peskio MCP Server

Peskio's public, read-only MCP server gives AI assistants tide predictions, wind and sea forecasts, water-clarity
estimates and estimated fish activity for 48,000+ coastal places across Europe, North Africa and US coasts, in
15 languages. No account, API key or sign-in is required.

## Tools

- **places_search** – resolve a town, beach, harbour or cove name to Peskio place IDs.
- **places_get_profile** – tide regime, coast exposure and shelter by wind/swell direction.
- **tides_get_table** – tide times, heights and coefficient estimates for up to two weeks.
- **conditions_get_day** – one day's fish activity score, per-discipline verdict, tide, sun, moon, wind and sea.
- **conditions_compare_days** – rank up to seven days by fish activity, verdict or water clarity.
- **spots_find_best** – rank nearby places within 5–25 km for a day and discipline.

## Example prompts

- "Best time to fish at Cadaqués this weekend?"
- "When is high tide in Saint-Malo tomorrow, and what is the coefficient?"
- "Where near Barcelona is best for shore fishing tomorrow?"

Tide coefficients are Peskio estimates, not official values. GO / marginal / NO ratings are for planning and do not
assess whether an outing is safe.
