InboxMCP is a hosted service operated by Logike SAS, the company behind Breakcold. Connect with OAuth 2.1 and PKCE. An InboxMCP account with a connected messaging account is required; a free trial is available, followed by a paid subscription. The hosted implementation is proprietary.
