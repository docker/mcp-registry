# PriceSway

Pricing sums for online stores, worked out from the numbers in your question.

Docs: https://pricesway.com/mcp-server

- **Profit per sale**: profit, margin, the most an ad can cost a sale and break-even return on ad spend.
- **Price from a competitor pricing rule**: match, or a percentage or amount below or above the cheapest, middle or highest competitor price, held between a lowest and highest markup on cost.
- **Effect of a price change**: an estimate of how units sold, revenue and gross profit are likely to move.

Two more tools return facts about PriceSway itself, which tracks competitor prices for online stores.

No account or key is needed. Every tool is read-only and nothing you send is stored. The server looks up no store, competitor or live price: you supply the numbers.
