Phasoric is a proprietary hosted service. This catalog entry does not distribute or license the Phasoric implementation; the service remains subject to its own terms.

Phasoric connects AI clients to explicitly authorized vaults containing Markdown notes. The hosted endpoint supports Streamable HTTP with OAuth; users sign in to Phasoric and approve access to their connected knowledge.

Only explicitly mapped, internet-reachable vaults are available through the hosted endpoint. Local-only vaults are not exposed. Write operations remain subject to Phasoric's preview, approval, and vault permissions.
