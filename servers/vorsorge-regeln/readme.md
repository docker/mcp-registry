# Vorsorge-Regeln

Answers German estate planning questions with the legal source for every value, using the same calculators as vorsorge-regeln.de: the notary fee for a will, a joint (Berlin) will, an inheritance contract or a power of attorney under the court and notary fees act (GNotKG); the statutory inheritance shares and compulsory portions under the BGB; fees and deadlines of the Central Register of Lasting Powers of Attorney, will custody and inheritance with statute, official link and check date; and the official emergency folder (Notfallmappe) of a German district or city. Answers are in German. It never asks for names, birth dates or health data and stores nothing.

## Tools

- `notarkosten_berechnen`: notary fee under the GNotKG
- `erbquoten_berechnen`: statutory inheritance shares and compulsory portions
- `vorsorge_fakten`: sourced fees, deadlines and rules by topic
- `notfallmappe_finden`: official emergency folders by district, city or state

All tools are read-only. The server is public: no account, no login and no API key.
