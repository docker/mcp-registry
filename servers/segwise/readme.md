Segwise is a hosted MCP service for advertising and creative-performance analytics. A Segwise account with access to an eligible organization and connected analytics data is required. Sign in with your Segwise account using OAuth 2.1 with PKCE; tools are discovered after authentication.

The service can list accessible organizations, start an analysis, and retrieve its result. Starting an analysis creates a temporary query job and may save conversation history. It cannot change campaigns, budgets, or creative assets, or generate new creatives.

For account access, security concerns, or customer-specific issues, use your existing Segwise support channel or the [Segwise Help Center](https://help.segwise.ai). Keep credentials and customer data out of public issues.
