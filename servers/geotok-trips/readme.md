# GeoTok Trips MCP Server

GeoTok Trips is a free, no-auth remote MCP server for planning trips inside AI assistants.

## Tools

- **search_flights** – airport codes and links to compare fares (Aviasales, Kiwi.com, Skyscanner, Google Flights).
- **search_hotels** – real hotels near a neighbourhood or landmark, with booking links for your dates.
- **search_transfers** – airport ↔ hotel by transit, walking or car, plus pre-booked transfer links.
- **find_things_to_do** – sights, museums and parks, with tour links.
- **find_food_spots** – restaurants people saved from TikTok, Instagram and YouTube food videos, verified on the map, with the dish to order.
- **plan_trip** – everything above in one call for a day-by-day itinerary.
- **save_trip_to_geotok** – saves the places as a trip link that opens in the GeoTok iPhone app, which reminds you when you're nearby.
